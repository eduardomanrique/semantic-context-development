# Initial Product Questions

The project is at semantic initialization stage. Some questions have now been partially resolved by story 1. The remaining questions block later behavior beyond claim creation and submission:

1. Are claimants limited to employees only beyond the first story, or will contractors and delegates also be allowed later?
2. What categories of expenses are in scope at launch, and are category values controlled by policy?
3. What evidence is required for each category once attachments are introduced?
4. Is approval single-step or multi-step?
5. Are policy limits based on amount, category, department, geography, or project?
6. Is currency implicit for the system or should it be captured explicitly on each claim?
7. Can a submitted claim later be withdrawn, canceled, or returned for edits?
8. How is reimbursement paid: payroll, accounts payable, bank transfer, or another method?
9. What external systems must be integrated at launch?
10. What compliance or retention requirements apply to receipts and decision history?
